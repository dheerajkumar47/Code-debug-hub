# Open BidSmith on your phone (anywhere, free)

**Same Wi-Fi as the laptop:** open `http://<laptop-ip>:8000` (the `start.bat` window shows it as
"Dashboard on your phone").

**Anywhere (mobile data, other Wi-Fi):**

1. Double-click **`start.bat`** as usual and leave it open.
2. Double-click **`phone.bat`**. The first time, it downloads Cloudflare Tunnel (about 1 minute).
3. In the window, find the line with **`https://something-random.trycloudflare.com`**.
4. Open that link on your phone → log in with **owner** + your dashboard password.
5. Chrome **⋮ → Add to Home screen** so it's one tap next time.

Good to know:
- The link **changes every time** you start `phone.bat`. Open the new one each time.
- The laptop must stay **on, plugged in, with both windows open** (lid-close setting: "Do nothing").
- The link is https and password-protected. Don't share it.
